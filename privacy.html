Privacy Policy

Yaadein · Last updated October 29, 2026

Yaadein is provided by Golu. This policy explains how the app handles information when you use its notes, diary, planning, reminders, snippets and subscription features. For privacy questions, contact lakshay01mudgal@gmail.com.

Your personal content

Your notes, diary entries, selected photos, moods, tasks, goals, time records, snippets and preferences are stored on your device. Yaadein does not require an app account and does not upload this content to a developer-operated server. There is no live cloud sync. Your device's system backup may include app data, depending on your settings and operating system.

Subscriptions and billing

Yaadein uses Google Play or, where available, Apple's App Store to process payments, and RevenueCat to verify purchases, restore subscriptions and determine access. Billing processing includes an app-generated anonymous user identifier, purchase and transaction information, subscription status, and relevant app, platform and store information. RevenueCat also provides subscription reporting. This processing does not send your notes or diary content to RevenueCat.

The purchasing store handles your payment details; Yaadein does not receive your full payment-card information. Billing providers process information under their own policies: Google Privacy Policy, Apple Privacy Policy, and RevenueCat Privacy Policy.

Permissions and features you control

  • Notifications support reminders. You can control notification permission in device settings.
  • Photos and files you select are used for diary images or backup import/export. Exporting or sharing content sends it to the location or app you choose.
  • Widgets display notes you choose and may be visible to people who can view your home screen.
  • The optional Android floating snippet bubble uses display-over-other-apps permission and a foreground service. It lets you select saved text; it does not read other apps' screens. Copying a snippet places it on the system clipboard, where operating-system rules determine access.
  • Internet access supports subscription purchases and verification, managed paywalls, and store update checks.

Backups and security

Exported Yaadein backups are encrypted with a password you choose. Keep both the password and backup safe; forgotten backup passwords cannot be recovered by us. Local app content relies on device and operating-system protections. Billing verification data uses platform-specific protected storage. No storage or transmission method can guarantee absolute security.

Retention and deletion

Local content remains on your device until you delete it using available app controls or remove the app's local data. On Android, clearing app storage removes local app data. Uninstalling normally removes local app data, but system backups and exported files may remain and can restore content later. Delete those copies separately through your backup provider or file storage.

Deleting app data does not cancel a subscription or delete transaction records held by the store or RevenueCat. Those providers may retain records for billing, fraud prevention and legal obligations. Contact us with privacy or deletion requests concerning billing information we can access; we may need information to locate the relevant record. We cannot remotely retrieve or delete personal content stored only on your device.

Support and sharing

If you email us, we receive your email address and the information you choose to send. We use it to respond to your request and retain correspondence as needed to handle the issue or meet legal obligations. Avoid sending sensitive diary content. We do not sell your personal content. The app does not include advertising SDKs; subscription providers and services you choose for backups or sharing handle the information needed for those functions.

Changes and contact

We may update this policy when the app or its data practices change. The date above identifies the latest revision. Contact Golu at lakshay01mudgal@gmail.com with questions or privacy requests.
